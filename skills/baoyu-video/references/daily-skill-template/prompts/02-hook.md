Hand-drawn notebook style infographic in 3:4 portrait ratio.

# Visual Design
Style: hand-drawn notebook / journal / doodle
Background: warm off-white cream #F5F3EF with very subtle paper texture
Text: bold hand-written brush style, black #1A1A1A
Highlights: colored marker/highlighter strokes behind key words
Decorations: simple line-art doodles

# Composition

# Decorative Elements
- Hand-drawn arrows pointing to key parts of the sketch
- Small sparkle marks near important concepts
- Highlighter strokes are messy/organic, not precise rectangles
- Light doodle icons related to the topic scattered in margins
- Generous whitespace, content centered, not cluttered
- Maybe a small hand-drawn exclamation mark or question mark for emphasis
