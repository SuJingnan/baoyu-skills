Hand-drawn notebook style list card in 3:4 portrait ratio.

# Visual Design
Style: hand-drawn notebook / journal / doodle
Background: warm off-white cream #F5F3EF with very subtle paper texture
Text: bold hand-written brush style for title, clean hand-written for list items
Highlights: colored marker/highlighter strokes
Decorations: simple line-art doodles

# Layout: List
Title: "{PAIN_TITLE}" in large bold hand-written black text
The title has a hand-drawn strikethrough or red #EF4444 marker underline for emphasis

List items (each on its own line with space between):
{PAIN_LIST}
Each item has:
- A hand-drawn "X" mark in red/pink marker (#F5C5C5 or #EF4444)
- Text in medium hand-written style, black #1A1A1A
- Key pain words highlighted with pink #F5C5C5 highlighter stroke
- Small doodle icon next to each item (sad face, broken chain, clock, etc.)

Bottom summary:
"{PAIN_SUMMARY}" in bold hand-written text
Background: light pink #F5C5C5 highlighter band behind the entire line

# Decorative Elements
- Hand-drawn "X" marks are slightly messy, not perfect crosses
- Small warning triangles or exclamation doodles in margins
- Arrows connecting related pain points
- Generous spacing between list items (like a real notebook)
- Maybe a small sad face doodle or broken heart sketch
- Total whitespace: 40%+

**Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait
